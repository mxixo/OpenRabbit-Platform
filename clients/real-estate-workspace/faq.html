FAQ

What happens on first launch?OpenRabbit opens in a Ready to Connect state. No sample data is presented as live data.
How do I connect services?Use the connection controls for Gmail, Calendar, CRM, social accounts and other supported integrations.
What is the demo below?It is a clearly labeled showcase of what the same workspace looks like after integrations are connected.
Does OpenRabbit take actions automatically?Analysis can run automatically; external writes stay approval-gated unless the user explicitly enables trusted automation.
How does Market Intelligence work?The map can use Google Maps for address/place search. Listings and market statistics should only populate from connected authoritative data sources.
