# Real-Estate-First Product Strategy

**Status:** Accepted product direction  
**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** First commercial proof of the OpenRabbit AI Operating Environment

## Decision

OpenRabbit will prove its operating-environment model through a heavily real-estate-oriented product before expanding to other industries. The first customer-visible loop is **lead to decision-ready deal**.

The product must answer five questions immediately after sign-in:

1. What changed?
2. What matters today?
3. What did OpenRabbit handle?
4. What needs approval?
5. What happens next?

Consequential actions are approval-first. New organizations configure their authority boundary, real-estate operating focus, and initial systems of record before OpenRabbit acts on live business information.

## First proof loop

`capture/enrich → prioritize/qualify → underwrite → approve/act → measure/learn`

This loop covers lead intake, property and contact enrichment, confidence-aware prioritization, qualification, scenario underwriting, approval-ready outreach or system changes, and outcome measurement.

## Product principles

- Sell a complete revenue outcome, not a catalog of AI workers.
- Keep workers behind the experience unless their responsibility or authority matters to the user.
- Never fabricate activity when live systems are not connected.
- Separate evidence, recommendation, proposed action, approval, execution, and outcome.
- Prefer excellent integrations with email/calendar, one CRM, one property-data provider, and one communication provider over a shallow integration catalog.
- Treat data provenance, confidence, actor attribution, idempotency, tenant isolation, and durable audit as product features.
- The default screen is an operating brief, not a generic dashboard or chat window.
- Other Industry Packs remain an architectural capability, not a near-term product priority.

## Initial command center

The signed-in experience prioritizes:

- approval queue;
- operating summary;
- changed/important/recommended/next panels;
- completed work and audit trail;
- first-proof-loop visibility;
- honest connection state when authoritative data is unavailable.

## Competitive position

Specialist real-estate products provide tools to operate. OpenRabbit coordinates existing tools, explains recommendations, and executes approved work with evidence and accountability. It should not attempt to win through raw feature count.

## Success measures

- Speed to first qualified response
- Contact and qualification rate by source
- Lead-to-appointment and lead-to-offer conversion
- Approval latency
- Stale-opportunity recovery
- Underwriting prediction versus realized outcome
- Cost per qualified opportunity and closed deal
- Recommendation acceptance and downstream result
- Time saved without unauthorized actions

## Near-term implementation order

1. Approval-first command center and setup decisions.
2. Durable approval, audit, worker, and organization state.
3. Live lead-to-deal intake with one CRM and email/calendar.
4. Property-data enrichment with provenance and confidence.
5. Qualification and underwriting recommendations.
6. Approval-gated communication and CRM writes.
7. Outcome telemetry and closed-loop learning.
