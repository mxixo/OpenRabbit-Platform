# Four-Day Production & Commercial Readiness Push

## Day 1 — Control plane
Policy service; action receipts/provenance; connector contract; adversarial tests; autonomy/disclosure source of truth.

## Day 2 — Reliability
Tenant-isolation matrix; idempotency/retry tests; connector lifecycle; observability/error taxonomy; incident/rollback runbooks.

## Day 3 — Commercial surface
Disclosure placement; autonomy UX; privacy/retention inventory; billing readiness; counsel checklist; support/escalation.

## Day 4 — Release candidate
Readiness checklist; deployment/env audit; real-estate golden-path spec; adversarial regression; blocker register; release/no-release packet.

Commercial readiness also requires external provider approvals/credentials, legal review, privacy/security controls, monitoring, recovery, billing, support, incident response, and successful end-to-end tests. Third-party dependencies remain blockers rather than being marked complete.
