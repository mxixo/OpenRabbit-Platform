# Terms — AI & Automation Clauses (Working Draft)

**Status: product draft for legal counsel; not final legal terms.**

## AI-assisted service
The Service uses artificial intelligence and automated systems to generate content, analyze information, recommend actions, and, when enabled, perform actions through connected services.

## Accuracy and uncertainty
AI-generated outputs are probabilistic and may contain errors, omissions, outdated information, or unexpected results. Automated systems may misunderstand context or instructions. The Service does not warrant that AI outputs or automated actions will always be accurate, complete, appropriate, available, or error-free.

## Delegated authority
By connecting a third-party service and enabling a capability, the user authorizes the Service to access information and perform actions within the scopes the user grants, subject to OpenRabbit policy controls and the user's selected autonomy settings. Routine authorized actions may occur without a separate confirmation for each action.

## Permissions
Users can manage supported connections and autonomy settings. OpenRabbit may require additional confirmation for selected sensitive actions and may deny actions that exceed granted authority, violate hard platform boundaries, or present unacceptable security/integrity risk.

## Third-party services
Connected services are operated by third parties. Their availability, data, permissions, APIs, terms, security, and functionality are outside OpenRabbit's complete control and may change.

## Audit and provenance
OpenRabbit may maintain activity, policy, provenance, and provider-verification records to operate, secure, debug, and explain automated workflows, subject to the applicable Privacy Policy and retention rules.

## Human judgment
Users should apply appropriate judgment to consequential decisions. Use of automation does not transfer the user's legal, contractual, professional, fiduciary, regulatory, or account responsibilities to OpenRabbit except where applicable law expressly provides otherwise.

## Separate regulated products
Features involving trading, investment activity, payments, regulated professional services, or other specially regulated activities may be governed by additional terms, disclosures, eligibility requirements, controls, or separate products.

## Legal review required
Final limitation-of-liability, indemnity, warranty, dispute-resolution, privacy, and jurisdiction-specific clauses must be drafted or approved by qualified counsel before commercial release.
